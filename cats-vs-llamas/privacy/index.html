Cats vs Llamas by Useful Llama — Privacy Policy

Version 1.3. Effective date: 21 November 2026. Changes in 1.3: optional gift codes through Useful Llama's code service, described in full. Changes in 1.2: achievements through Google Play Games. Changes in 1.1: optional private feedback sent to Useful Llama through Google Forms, and a one-time thank-you for the first one.

Cats vs Llamas is a game provided by the independent developer trading as Useful Llama. In this policy, “we” means that developer. Privacy and support contact: useful.llamita@gmail.com.

This policy explains how Cats vs Llamas handles information. The game is meant for players aged 13 and over.

Your game on your phone

Your progress is saved in the game's private storage on your phone: your side, your characters and the names you give them, your city, items, diamonds and treats, settings, and the times used for daily gifts, raids and timers. No Useful Llama account is needed and we do not run a server that stores your game. The game does not ask for your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or device location.

Optional Google Play Games sign-in, cloud save and achievements

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google tells the game your Play Games player name, which the game shows in Settings. While you are signed in, the game saves a copy of your progress (the same game save described above) to Google Play Games' saved games, so you can get it back on a new phone. That copy is stored by Google in your own Google account; we cannot see it. While you are signed in, the achievements you earn in the game (for example First Victory or Nap Time) are also unlocked as Google Play Games achievements on your Play Games profile, where Google shows them under its own settings; achievements earned before you signed in are unlocked when you sign in. Nothing about them is sent to us. Signing in is optional and the game works the same without it. Google handles sign-in, saved games and achievements under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage, turn off or delete your Play Games data, including this game's saved game and achievements, in the Google Play Games app or your Google account. See how to delete your data.

Optional rewarded ads

The game never shows an ad you did not choose. Some rewards can be earned by choosing to watch an ad. Google AdMob provides those ads. Its Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location, device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app-set ID, ad interaction information and diagnostic information, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

Google's User Messaging Platform checks whether your consent is needed where you live and, if so, shows Google's consent form and stores your choices on the phone. Where available, the game lets you review those choices again. Android's settings also let you reset or delete your advertising ID. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Google Play purchases

Optional items can be bought through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment and your payment details; the game never receives your card number. The game asks Google Play which items you own so it can give them to you, and keeps a record on the phone of what it has given. Google keeps purchase records under its own policies.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game asks your phone to show a notification when a raid on your city begins while you are away. These are set up on the phone itself; nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.

Optional gift codes

If you choose to redeem a gift code, the app sends the code, the app identifier and a randomly generated installation identifier to Useful Llama's code service on Google Apps Script. No Useful Llama account is required. The installation identifier lets the service recognise a repeated request and manage the redemption limit; it is not your name, but it can link requests from this installation. Checking an existing gift sends its grant reference and the same installation identifier instead of the code.

Useful Llama keeps redemption records in a private Google spreadsheet, including the app, installation identifier, grant reference, receipt, redemption time, code version, allocated place, grant status and notification status. The service's code list stores salted hashes rather than readable codes. Redemption notifications sent to the Useful Llama owner include the app, reward, receipt, time and redemption counts. They do not include the code or installation identifier. Google processes service and connection information under its own policies.

These records provide and check the gift, prevent repeated requests from consuming another place, enforce the redemption limit and manage owner notifications. Uninstalling the app does not delete the service's redemption records or emails. To ask for access to your redemption record or for its removal, write to useful.llamita@gmail.com with the app name and the receipt or approximate redemption time. Do not send the gift code, private recovery reference or passwords. When a record is removed, its installation identifier is deleted and the redemption still counts towards the code's limit. The gift can no longer be checked or restored from the service, but a horse and horse armour already given in the game stay in your Cats' gear. We may need further information to locate the record. Google controls its own service records.

On the phone, the game keeps the gift's record (the installation identifier, grant reference, receipt and redemption time) in its private storage, apart from Google Play purchases, so the gift is never given twice.

Optional private feedback

If you choose Feedback in Settings, the game sends your 1–5 star answer, any note you write and the game's name and version to a Google Form operated by Useful Llama, over an encrypted connection. This is feedback to the developer, not a Google Play rating or review, and you can play without sending it. The game does not attach your name, email, account, device identifier or game progress. Your note may still contain personal information, so please leave out passwords, payment details and anything else sensitive. Google processes the submission and connection information, such as your IP address, under its own policies.

The first feedback you send earns a one-time gift of diamonds, whatever the stars; the game notes on your phone that it was given. Responses are stored in Google Forms and a linked Google Sheet, emailed to Useful Llama's inbox and included in a monthly internal report. They are read by the people who run Useful Llama and are not published. We keep feedback while it helps us improve the game, then delete or anonymise it. To find, correct or delete feedback you sent, email us with the app, the rough date and some of your words.

Other Useful Llama apps

Settings can show a list of other Useful Llama apps with a link to each one's Google Play page. The game does not check which apps you have installed. Opening a link lets Google Play handle the visit under its own policies. Nothing is given for installing another app.

Backups

Android may include the game's save in your phone's own backup (for example, to your Google account), depending on your phone and settings. That backup, and the Google Play Games cloud save if you are signed in, also holds a gift code's record (the installation identifier, grant reference and receipt), so a restored game keeps the gift without using the code again. That backup is handled by your phone's backup provider; we do not receive it.

Children

Cats vs Llamas is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used a feature that sent information, contact us and we will help.

Keeping and deleting information

Information on your phone stays until you delete it: uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, removes your local save. Copies in Google Play Games or your phone's backup are managed in those services. Gift-code redemption records kept by Useful Llama's code service stay until you ask for their removal, as described under Optional gift codes. Our deletion page explains each step. If you email us, we keep the email until your question is resolved.

Your choices and requests

Email useful.llamita@gmail.com with any privacy question or request. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to complain to a privacy regulator. We hold very little about you: we cannot reach the save on your phone, we keep gift-code redemption records only if you redeem a code, and Google holds the Play Games, ads and purchase information under its own policies.

External services and this website

Google and other services you use may process information in countries outside your own. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to provide and secure it. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the game changes how it handles information, and change the effective date above.
