Delete your Cats vs Llamas data

Cats vs Llamas by Useful Llama keeps your game on your phone. There is no Useful Llama account. The only copy outside your phone is the optional Google Play Games cloud save, which Google keeps in your own Google account.

The game on your phone

Uninstall Cats vs Llamas, or open your phone's Settings, then Apps, then Cats vs Llamas, then Storage, and tap Clear storage. This deletes your whole save: characters, city, items and settings.

The Google Play Games cloud save

Open the Google Play Games app, go to its settings, and use Google's option to delete Play Games data; you can delete the data of one game, such as Cats vs Llamas, or all of it. Uninstall the game first if you don't want a new copy to be saved.

By email

If you need help, email useful.llamita@gmail.com with the subject "Cats vs Llamas - delete my data". We hold no game data about you on our side, so we will explain the steps above for your phone and reply within 30 days.

Gift-code records

If you redeemed a gift code, Useful Llama's code service keeps a redemption record. To have it removed, email useful.llamita@gmail.com with the app name and the receipt or approximate redemption time (never the code itself). Its installation identifier is then deleted; the redemption still counts towards the code's limit, and the horse and horse armour already given stay in your game.

What is kept

  • Nothing by us: we never receive your save. The only exception is a gift-code redemption record, kept if you redeem a code, until you ask for its removal (above).
  • Google keeps its own records of purchases, and of ads you chose to watch, under the Google Privacy Policy.
