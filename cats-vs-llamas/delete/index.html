Delete your Cats vs Llamas data

Cats vs Llamas by Useful Llama keeps your game on your phone. There is no Useful Llama account. Copies of your game can also exist in the optional Google Play Games cloud save and in your phone's own backup. Useful Llama itself only holds feedback you chose to send and, if you redeemed one, a gift-code record. Each is covered below.

The game on your phone

Uninstall Cats vs Llamas, or open your phone's Settings, then Apps, then Cats vs Llamas, then Storage, and tap Clear storage. This deletes your whole save: characters, city, items and settings.

The Google Play Games cloud save

Open the Google Play Games app, go to its settings, and use Google's option to delete Play Games data; you can delete the data of one game, such as Cats vs Llamas, or all of it. Uninstall the game first if you don't want a new copy to be saved.

Your phone's backup

Android may include the game's save in your phone's own backup (for example, to your Google account). That backup is handled by your phone's backup provider, not by us. To remove it, delete the backup in your phone's or Google account's backup settings.

Feedback you sent

If you used Feedback in Settings, your star answer, any note you wrote and the game's version are kept in Useful Llama's Google Form, its linked Google Sheet, our inbox and a monthly internal report. To have it deleted, email useful.llamita@gmail.com with the subject "Cats vs Llamas - delete my feedback", the rough date and some of your words so we can find it. We will delete it from each of those places and reply within 30 days. The one-time diamond gift for feedback stays in your game.

Gift-code records

If you redeemed a gift code, Useful Llama's code service keeps a redemption record. To have it removed, email useful.llamita@gmail.com with the app name and the receipt or approximate redemption time (never the code itself). Its installation identifier is then deleted; the redemption still counts towards the code's limit, and the horse and horse armour already given stay in your game.

Help by email

If you need help with any of these steps, email useful.llamita@gmail.com with the subject "Cats vs Llamas - delete my data". We will reply within 30 days.

What is kept

  • We never receive your game save. We keep only feedback you chose to send, until you ask for its deletion or it is no longer needed to improve the game (then deleted or anonymised), and a gift-code redemption record, kept if you redeem a code until you ask for its removal (above). If you email us, we keep the email until your question is resolved.
  • Google keeps its own records of purchases, and of ads you chose to watch, under the Google Privacy Policy.

See also the privacy policy.
