Crimson Defiance by Useful Llama — Privacy Policy

Version 1.3. Effective date: 21 November 2026. Changes in 1.3: optional gift codes through Useful Llama's code service. Changes in 1.2: the optional cloud save and achievements through Google Play Games. Changes in 1.1: optional private feedback through Google Forms.

Crimson Defiance is a strategy game provided by the independent developer trading as Useful Llama. In this policy, “we” means that developer. Privacy and support contact: useful.llamita@gmail.com. Terms of use: https://useful-llama.github.io/crimson-defiance/terms/.

In short: there are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no Useful Llama accounts. Your game stays on your phone. Information leaves your phone only when you choose to play over the internet, share a map by code, send us feedback, sign in to Google Play Games (cloud save and achievements), redeem a gift code, or buy the full game through Google Play.

Your game on your phone

Your campaign progress and stars, scenario scores, saved games, settings, the maps you make in the Map Editor and the names you type (your player name and map names) are saved in the game's private storage on your phone. The game also keeps a small record of its own loadings on the phone (how long each took and how much memory the phone had), so it can recover after Android closes it; that record never leaves the phone unless you copy or share it yourself. If you redeem a gift code, the game also keeps the gift's grant reference, receipt and redemption time and a randomly generated installation identifier in its private storage, apart from your purchase; it never keeps the code itself. No Useful Llama account is needed and we do not run a server that stores your game.

The game does not ask for your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location, and it does not use the advertising ID.

No ads, no analytics

Crimson Defiance shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software.

Google Play purchases

The first missions and some skirmish maps are free. The rest of the game is opened by one optional purchase, “the full game”, through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the payment and your payment details; the game never receives your card number. The game asks Google Play whether this purchase belongs to your Google account, so it can open the full game and restore it on a new phone, and keeps a note of the answer on the phone. Google keeps purchase records under the Google Privacy Policy.

Optional gift codes

If you choose to redeem a gift code, the app sends the code, the app identifier and a randomly generated installation identifier to Useful Llama's code service on Google Apps Script. No Useful Llama account is required. The installation identifier lets the service recognise a repeated request and manage the redemption limit; it is not your name, but it can link requests from this installation. Checking an existing gift sends its grant reference and the same installation identifier instead of the code.

Useful Llama keeps redemption records in a private Google spreadsheet, including the app, installation identifier, grant reference, receipt, redemption time, code version, allocated place, grant status and notification status. The service's code list stores salted hashes rather than readable codes. Redemption notifications sent to the Useful Llama owner include the app, reward, receipt, time and redemption counts. They do not include the code or installation identifier. Google processes service and connection information under its own policies.

These records provide and check the gift, prevent repeated requests from consuming another place, enforce the redemption limit and manage owner notifications. Uninstalling the app does not delete the service's redemption records or emails. To ask for access to your redemption record or for its removal, write to useful.llamita@gmail.com with the app name and the receipt or approximate redemption time. Do not send the gift code, private recovery reference or passwords. When a record is removed, its installation identifier is deleted; the redemption still counts towards the code's limit, and the gift can no longer be checked or restored from the service (a later check on the phone removes it). We may need further information to locate the record. Google controls its own service records.

Playing on the same Wi-Fi (LAN)

When you host or join a game on your local Wi-Fi, the phones talk to each other directly on that network. A hosting phone announces “your player name’s game” to the other phones on the same Wi-Fi so they can find it. During the match the phones send each other the game's commands. None of this goes through us or over the internet.

Optional internet play

If you choose MULTIPLAYER, INTERNET, the phones need a short introduction before they can connect to each other. For this the game uses Google Firebase (a Realtime Database and an anonymous sign-in, with no account and no sign-in screen), run by us for this purpose:

  • the hosting phone saves a room under a short game code: the player name you chose, the game's settings, the game's version and the number of open seats;
  • each joining phone saves its player name and its connection offer under that room, and the host's phone answers it. Connection offers contain technical network information, including IP addresses, so the phones can reach each other;
  • the room and everything under it is deleted when the host stops hosting.

The match itself then runs phone to phone. To find a route between the phones, Google's public STUN servers see your phone's IP address. Firebase and STUN are provided by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Choose a player name that does not identify you if you prefer: other players see it.

Optional map sharing

If you share a map from the Map Editor by code, the map and its name are saved in the same Firebase database under a six-character code, so anyone who types that code can download it. Shared maps are kept so the code keeps working. To have a shared map removed, email us its code. Sharing a map as a file or text through your phone's share sheet sends it only where you send it.

Fonts

When your phone is online, the game may load its text font from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of that request. The game works offline without it.

Optional feedback

If you choose Send feedback, the game sends your 1–5 star answer, your optional note and the game's name and version to a private Google Form operated by Useful Llama, which emails each answer to Useful Llama's inbox. This is feedback to the developer, not a Google Play rating or review, and you can play without ever sending it. The game does not attach your name, email address, account or any device identifier. Your note could still contain personal information, so please leave out passwords, payment details and other sensitive information.

Google processes the submission, and technical connection information such as your IP address, under its own policies. We use feedback to find problems and improve our apps. Answers are kept in Google Forms, a linked Google Sheet and a monthly internal report emailed to Useful Llama; they are not published. We keep them while we need them to act on them, normally no longer than twelve months. To have feedback found, corrected or deleted, email us with the game's name, the approximate date and enough of your note to find it. Uninstalling the game does not delete feedback already sent.

Other Useful Llama apps

Settings can show a list of other Useful Llama apps with a link to each one's Google Play page. The game does not check which apps you have installed. Opening a link lets Google Play handle the visit under its own policies.

Cloud save and achievements (Google Play Games)

Cloud save. If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game keeps a copy of your progress (campaign, scenario scores, achievements and settings) in your Google Play Games saved games, so it can be brought back on another phone. Google stores this copy with your Google account; Useful Llama does not receive it. You can delete it in Google Play Games' settings, and the game works without signing in.

Achievements. When you are signed in, the achievements you earn (for example winning a campaign mission or a skirmish, or how many enemy units you have destroyed) are unlocked on your Google Play Games profile, where Google shows them under its own settings. Achievements earned before you signed in are unlocked when you sign in. To count them, the game keeps a small record on your phone (the achievements earned and the totals of units destroyed and trained, buildings destroyed and skirmishes won), which goes into the cloud save with your progress.

Signing in is optional; the game remembers nothing about your account beyond the Play Games name it shows in Settings. Google Play Games processes your sign-in, saved games and achievements under the Google Privacy Policy.

Backups

Android may include the game's save in your phone's own backup (for example, to your Google account), depending on your phone and settings. That backup also holds a gift code's information (the installation identifier, grant reference and receipt), so a phone restored from it keeps the gift without using the code again. That backup is handled by your phone's backup provider; we do not receive it.

Children

Crimson Defiance is a war strategy game for teenagers and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used internet play or map sharing, contact us and we will help remove the information.

Keeping and deleting information

Information on your phone stays until you delete it: uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, removes your saves, maps and settings. The cloud save and achievements are managed in Google Play Games' settings. Internet-play rooms are deleted when the host stops hosting. Shared maps are removed on request (email us the code). Gift-code redemption records are kept and removed as described under Optional gift codes. If you email us, we keep the email until your question is resolved.

We hold very little about you: we cannot reach the saves on your phone, we keep gift-code redemption records only as described above, and Google holds purchase information under its own policies.
