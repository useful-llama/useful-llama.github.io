Diet & DEXA: the whole-photo food check

These two small files are the part of the Diet & DEXA app that decides whether a photo shows any food at all. They were trained in part on front-of-packet photos from Open Food Facts, shared by its contributors under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 licence, so we share these files under the same licence. The photos themselves are not included. Brand names and packet artwork belong to their owners.
