Delete your Diet & DEXA: The Full Picture data

Diet & DEXA by Useful Llama keeps your diary on your phone. The only account is the optional Back up with Google sign-in. Here is how to delete your data and that account.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Delete your Google backup and its account: open the app, go to Settings, then Back up with Google, and tap Delete backup. This deletes the backup document and its Firebase sign-in account straight away.
  2. Delete everything on the phone: Settings, then Delete all my data. This removes your diary, meal photos, body scans, runs, learned meals and learned report layouts from the phone. Uninstalling the app also removes them.

By email (if you no longer have the app)

Email useful.llama@gmail.com with the subject "Diet and DEXA - delete my backup" from the Google account you backed up with (or tell us its address). We will delete the backup document and its Firebase sign-in account within 30 days and reply when it is done. We may ask you to confirm the request comes from you.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: the backup of your diary (meals, water, weights, body-scan results, exercise, fasting history, settings) and the sign-in account linked to it.
  • Not held by us: meal photos, runs and routes, and your Claude key were never in the backup. Anything you sent to Anthropic with your own Claude key is handled under your Anthropic account.
  • Kept: nothing. We keep no copy after deletion. Google Play keeps its own record of any purchase under its policies.
