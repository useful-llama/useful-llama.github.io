Helping the food recogniser learn

You can send a report when the phone names a food wrongly ("Wrong food?"). The app also sends a report when you log a meal Claude read, or change the foods your phone named before saving ("Help the food camera learn", on unless you turn it off in Settings). Reports contain the phone's guess, the foods you logged or typed, and lists of numbers made from the photo's look - never the photo. You agree that Useful Llamita may use them to train and improve the app's food recogniser. Details are in the privacy policy.
