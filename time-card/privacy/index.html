Time Card by Useful Llama — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 Changes on 27 October 2026: added how journey activities received from Road Camera are handled.

Time Card by Useful Llama is provided by the independent developer trading as Useful Llama. In this policy, “we” means that developer. Privacy and support contact: useful.llama@gmail.com.

This policy explains how Time Card handles information. The web version is available at Time Card Privacy Policy.

Information you enter

The app stores employee names, email addresses and phone numbers; employer names, contact-person names and email addresses; work arrangements, expected hours, usual finish times, overtime rules and multipliers, unpaid-break and check-in/out rounding settings, days marked as public holidays, periods, dates, activities, recorded times, notes and any signature you choose to include. It also stores drafts, issued time-card PDFs, reusable employee details, preferences, document allowances, rewarded-reset points, local purchase status and Terms acceptance records.

These details are used to prepare your time cards, suggest entries or reminders and create the files you request. Employee and employer contact details are copied into each card and can appear on its PDF. Issued cards keep their saved details when you later change an employee entry. Deleting an employee from the reusable list does not change existing drafts or issued cards.

Email drafts can include the employee's name, email and phone and a greeting to the employer or contact person. Entering an employee email does not change or verify the email app's sending account.

Check-in and check-out rounding

Optional rounding changes the saved check-in or check-out time according to your settings. When rounding changes a time, the app also retains its original device-clock time. These values are kept with drafts and issued cards, included in backups and shown in time-card PDFs. They are device records, not independently verified attendance or an authoritative time source. Existing shared files and older backups are not changed when you edit or delete local records.

Optional suburb at check-in

If you choose Use location, the app requests approximate device location to suggest the rough suburb at check-in or check-out. You can choose Time only, type a place yourself, switch location off on the check-in card or change the permission in Android settings. This feature is not continuous route tracking.

The device's lookup service may send your approximate position to its provider to find an area name. The provider depends on the device, and lookup is not necessarily performed only on your phone. An approximate position from the last ten minutes may be used. Automatic results are saved as “Near” followed by an area name; the feature does not save a street address or coordinate fallback. If no name is found, the time is saved without a suggested place. Check the suggestion before sharing it.

The app keeps original check-in/out times and places alongside editable values. Clearing the visible place alone does not remove its recorded original. Remove location data in a draft clears both visible and original places, keeps the recorded times and marks the removal. It does not change issued cards, existing PDFs or older backups. Typed places and older records may contain more precise details.

Places can appear in PDFs and backups. Edit markers compare recorded times and places with current values; they are not a complete edit history or independent proof of attendance. Description changes are not tracked by these markers.

Optional reminders and suggestions

You can enable a local check-out reminder. The app stores its chosen time, a reference to the related check-in and a label with the check-in time and activity. A usual finish time or expected hours can suggest a reminder time, which you can change. A reminder does not automatically check you out.

The app requests notification permission where required. Android settings and battery restrictions can delay or prevent notifications. A generic public preview is supplied without activity details; actual visibility on the lock screen or other notification surfaces depends on your device settings.

The app clears the stored reminder time, label and check-in reference when the reminder fires or is cancelled. This does not erase the time card or Android notification history. Checking out cancels the reminder. After a restart, the app checks that the related check-in remains open before restoring or showing a saved reminder. Scheduled reminders are excluded from app backups.

The app keeps a separate local history of up to 100 activity descriptions for suggestions, including descriptions from drafts and issued cards. Deleting a card, draft or PDF does not erase its saved description from that history. The history and hidden-suggestions list are included in backups. The cross beside a suggestion hides it; it does not delete the saved description or the activity on a time card. Using that activity again can bring its suggestion back. Clear saved activity suggestions on the home screen deletes the saved suggestion history and hidden-suggestion entries after you confirm. Activities on existing drafts and time cards stay unchanged. Merely reopening the suggestions does not refill the cleared history from those records; later check-ins or issued cards can save descriptions again. Previously exported backups are unchanged, and restoring an older backup may bring saved suggestions back. Saved descriptions can also be displaced from the limited history. Clearing all app data removes suggestions and other local records, so keep needed copies first.

Sharing selected employer details

Share employer details creates a file from selected entries and fields: employer, contact person, employer email, work arrangement, expected hours, period, overtime rules, unpaid-break and rounding settings, with optional employee name, email and phone. Employee information starts unticked; Select all can include it. Review the final selection before sending.

Changing or deleting details locally does not remove copies already shared or imported by someone else.

Optional writing and speech helpers

Smart helpers start switched off and can be changed in Settings. On supported devices and languages, the summary helper uses Google's on-device AI to turn activity descriptions and dates from the current card into suggested notes. Long input may be shortened before processing, so a summary may omit activities. Review and edit the result before saving or sharing. Saved notes follow the same retention, backup and sharing practices as other time-card information.

The summary content is processed on-device and is not sent to Useful Llama. Model preparation or downloads may require a connection. Google's SDK collects usage and diagnostic information, including device and app details, identifiers, performance and feature events, under its policies. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Speak instead of type opens the speech-recognition service installed on your phone when you tap the microphone. That service processes microphone audio and returns text to the app. Depending on the service and its settings, audio may be sent to the provider's servers; this feature is not guaranteed to work entirely on-device. The provider controls its audio handling and retention. The app receives the recognised text for your activity or note, rather than recording an audio file itself. Check the returned words before saving or sharing them.

Turning a helper off does not erase text already saved on cards or copies already shared.

Google Play purchases

Google Play handles optional Unlimited yearly subscriptions, one-time purchases and payment information. The app queries purchase and subscription information to check and acknowledge access. It stores a local unlock status and, when needed, the time an ownership check first returned no purchase. It does not retain order IDs or purchase tokens, or receive your payment-card number. Google retains purchase information under its own policies.

Restoring a purchase does not restore your documents. Purchases and backups are separate for Invoice Maker and Time Card.

Optional rewarded ads

You can choose a rewarded ad for three more watermark-free creations when the allowance for that document kind is used. You can continue creating watermarked documents without watching ads. Ads are shown only when you choose the rewarded-ad option. That option is not shown when Unlimited is unlocked.

Google AdMob provides ads. Its Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location, device and account identifiers such as advertising and app-set IDs, interaction information and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This is separate from storing your documents locally. Ad-related approximate location can be inferred from an IP address without device location permission.

Google's User Messaging Platform checks applicable consent requirements and stores privacy choices on the device. For users without Unlimited, a consent-information refresh runs when the app starts after Terms acceptance, and a consent form may appear where required. The ad SDK is initialised and an ad requested when you choose the reward option. Consent checks themselves communicate with Google; not seeing an ad does not mean no information leaves the device.

Where available, Ad privacy choices lets you review or change your choices in How it works or the Unlimited details dialog. Android settings also provide advertising-ID controls. Changing a choice does not automatically delete information already held by Google. See Google's Privacy Policy.

The app stores issued-document counts and rewarded-reset points. These are included in app backups. Rewarding one document kind does not reset another kind or unlock Unlimited.

Journey activities received from Road Camera

When you choose to send journeys from Road Camera, Time Card receives dates, start and finish times, addresses and activity descriptions that may contain estimated distance, purpose and notes. It shows an import prompt before adding accepted activities to your current draft, or creating a draft if none exists. Existing activities are kept, and activities already on the draft are not added again. The imported activity data is stored with your time card and can be edited or removed there. It follows the same backup, export and sharing rules as other time-card activities. This transfer does not include video files or the full GPS route. Choosing Not now leaves your draft unchanged. Deleting a source journey in Road Camera does not remove the Time Card copy, and deleting a Time Card activity does not remove the original journey.

Email, sharing and imported files

When you choose Email, the app passes the attachment, recipient address, subject and proposed message to your chosen email app. You review and send the email there. The email app controls the actual sending account and may process or sync the draft and attachment before you send. This app does not send the email itself or confirm delivery.

CSV and Excel exports contain the selected invoice or time-card records and their included details. You choose whether to save them or open or share them with another service. Exporting a file does not itself connect the app to accounting or payroll software.

Opening, sharing or saving a file gives the receiving app or storage provider access to that copy. A selected folder may belong to a cloud service. Recipients can retain or forward files. Only share information you are authorised to disclose.

Opening a shared details attachment or selecting a file for import lets the app read that file and show its contents for review. A sender name inside a file is not verified identity. No details are added until you confirm the import. Copies held by recipients or external providers follow their own handling and retention practices.

Backups and security

Records are stored in the app's private device storage. No Useful Llama account is required, and the app does not automatically upload your document records to a Useful Llama server. This does not prevent the Google, lookup, email or other provider processing described in this policy.

The app disables Android's automatic app backup setting. Use the app's own backup/export controls to keep copies. App backups are ZIP files containing structured records and files; they are not encrypted. Protect your device and store backups somewhere you trust. Restoring can replace local records, so read the confirmation first.

Save all PDFs exports saved PDFs and any included receipt images in a ZIP that opens without the app. It is not a full backup of editable records. Its READ ME explains omissions and records without a saved PDF. A structured backup requires the matching app to restore.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you remove it with the relevant app controls, replace it during restore, or clear the app's local storage. Uninstalling can remove local data. Keep needed copies first.

Deleting a document does not necessarily remove reusable details, settings, allowance counts or Terms acceptance records. It also does not remove previously exported PDFs, backups, email drafts or messages, recipient copies or records held by external services. Manage those copies separately with their providers.

If you decline changed Terms, you can still view and share saved PDFs, export them and save a structured backup without accepting the new Terms or buying an upgrade. Drafts and records without saved PDFs are not displayed in that read-only viewer.

Support and privacy requests

Contact useful.llama@gmail.com with support or privacy questions. If you email us, we and our email provider receive your email address, message and attachments to handle the request. Include only information needed to resolve it. We keep support emails until the issue is resolved, then delete them from our mailbox. Our email provider may retain copies under its own retention and backup policies.

You can ask about access, correction or deletion of information we hold by emailing us. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent or complain to a privacy regulator. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase records held only on your device, or withdraw copies held by recipients.
