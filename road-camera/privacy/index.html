Who provides the app

Road Camera is provided by the independent developer trading as Useful Llama. Privacy and support contact: useful.llama@gmail.com. This policy is available at https://useful-llama.github.io/road-camera/privacy/.

Camera, microphone and recordings

The app uses camera access to record video and to provide previews and selected-frame analysis. Video may contain faces, vehicles, number plates and other identifying details. Sound recording starts off. If you enable it and grant microphone access, sound is recorded with the video. Speech typing is a separate microphone use described below.

Recording can continue through a foreground service while the app is not visible or the screen is off. A recording notification provides status and controls. Recording permissions and device restrictions still apply. Turning off video sound does not disable speech input that you separately choose.

Location, motion and events

With location permission, the app obtains position, speed, accuracy and related readings during recording, including while the screen is off. These can be stored with clips, used for stamps, event detection and speed-camera proximity, and included in reports. Motion sensors support impact and movement detection. The app stores event times, types and associated readings and clip references.

Smart event detection uses estimated speed and movement to flag clips for review. Optional AI-enhanced detection analyses reduced video frames on the phone to flag possible close calls. It does not independently confirm an accident. Detection settings can be changed in Settings.

Journeys and location-only logging

Road Camera stores journey start and end times, GPS route points and related readings, estimated distances and speeds, available start and end addresses, your purpose label and notes, and whether video was being recorded. Journeys can be logged alongside video or through location-only logging, which uses a location foreground service and can continue while the app is not visible or the screen is off. Location-only mode does not record video or sound. Only journeys meeting the app's duration and distance thresholds are retained as journey records.

When maps and address lookups are enabled, start and end coordinates may be sent through the phone's geocoding service to look up addresses. Loading a route map sends requests for its area to Google; the existing map and service-data disclosures apply. You can turn maps and address lookups off without deleting previously saved journey information.

Excel exports contain journey details, including coordinates, addresses and notes. Saving or sharing them creates copies outside the journey record. A temporary copy of the latest Excel file is kept in the app's cache for sharing; it is replaced when you create another file and removed when you delete a journey. When you choose Add to a time card, Road Camera sends selected journey dates, times, addresses and a description containing estimated distance, purpose and notes to Time Card by Useful Llama on your device. It does not send video files or the full GPS route through that action. Time Card asks before adding the activities to a draft. If Time Card is not installed, the app opens its store page; you must return and send again after installing.

When you choose to send travel to Invoice Maker by Useful Llama, Road Camera sends the journey date, a description containing start and end address summaries and any notes, estimated kilometres rounded to one decimal place, and whole-minute duration. It does not send video files or the full GPS route through this action. Invoice Maker asks you to review the lines, select an invoice type and optionally enter a rate before adding them to a draft. If Invoice Maker is not installed, its store page opens; return to Road Camera and send again after installation. Copies added to Invoice Maker are managed separately from Road Camera's journey records.

Deleting a journey removes its Road Camera record, not separate clips, exported copies or Time Card activities. Those copies have their own deletion controls and may be retained or shared separately. Uninstalling or clearing Road Camera's data removes app-held journey records; exported and transferred copies remain subject to their destinations' settings.

Car connection and driving reminders

If you enable car auto-start, Bluetooth access is used to identify the selected car connection and react to connection changes. The app stores your selected car and related settings.

The optional Driving reminder starts off. With Physical activity permission, Google Play services supplies activity transitions, such as travelling in a vehicle, so the app can offer a recording reminder when appropriate. This reminder uses activity recognition rather than continuous GPS tracking for its trigger. Google Play services and device services operate under their own privacy practices.

Reports and remembered details

Reports may contain your account, driver and witness contact details, licence and policy numbers, vehicle details, injury descriptions, locations, estimated speed and impact readings, photos, selected clips and AI suggestions. Your own driver details are remembered for future reports. Deleting an individual report does not necessarily clear those separately remembered details.

You choose which report sections and photos appear in the PDF. Unticking a section excludes it from that export; it does not delete it from the stored report. Only share information you are authorised to disclose.

On-device analysis and AI summaries

Selected clips can be analysed on the phone using object detection and text recognition to suggest vehicle descriptions, possible number plates and lighting conditions, and to select frames for report photos. Suggestions can be wrong. Accepted values and selected photos become part of the report. Video analysis is distinct from address lookup and map loading, which may communicate with external providers.

Quick report can read text from photos you take, using on-device text recognition to suggest names, phone numbers, licence numbers, vehicle plates and insurance details. It fills only empty fields; review every suggested value. The original photos are retained with the report, along with recognised field values and markers showing which values came from photos. Changing a field does not change the photo. Report deletion and exported-copy rules apply as described below. Google's text-recognition service may collect usage and diagnostic information as described in this policy.

On supported devices and languages, you can request an on-device AI summary of your account. The supplied text and generated summary are processed locally. Saved summaries are report content and may appear in the PDF if included. Model preparation or downloads can require a connection.

These analysis features do not upload your footage or account to Useful Llama. MediaPipe sends performance and usage metrics to Google. Google's ML Kit and on-device AI services also collect usage and diagnostic information, such as device and app details, identifiers, performance information and feature events, under Google's policies. Local content processing does not mean that no service data leaves the phone.

Speech input and guided questions

If you use speech typing or Talk me through it, microphone audio is passed to the phone's speech-recognition service. On-device recognition is used when supported; otherwise the installed service may send audio to its provider's servers. The provider controls its handling and retention. The app receives recognised text for your report rather than saving the dictation audio as a report audio file. This is separate from any sound recorded with video.

The guided flow presents questions one at a time and accepts spoken answers and navigation commands. You can pause listening and type instead. Recognised words and accepted suggestions are saved into the report for review.

Transcribing recorded clip audio

On compatible phones running Android 13 or later, you can transcribe sound already recorded in clips using the phone's on-device speech recogniser. The app supplies the clip's audio for local transcription; this feature does not use cloud speech recognition. It is separate from speech typing, which may use a provider's servers as described above. A speech-language pack may need downloading through the phone's service. Generated text, approximate audio-section times, clip references and your selections are stored with the report, including unselected lines. Some strong language is masked in the text; the original audio is not changed. Selected lines can appear in exported PDFs and be supplied to an on-device AI summary. Unticking a line does not delete it, remove it from an existing summary or recall exported copies. Delete the report to remove its app-held transcript; original clips and shared copies have their own deletion controls and retention.

Address lookup and maps

Looking up an address sends the relevant coordinates through the device's geocoding service to its provider. The guided report flow can also perform this lookup while preparing suggestions. A returned address may be saved in your report.

Loading the report's embedded Google map sends requests for the selected location to Google, even if you do not share the report. The Maps SDK also collects service information such as device metadata, IP address, SDK identifiers and diagnostic information. Opening an external map link gives its destination the selected location. You can move the report pin by tapping the map or dragging it. The report retains the position you choose separately from the original GPS position. In the guided report flow, moving the pin can trigger another address lookup. The map and address are suggestions to check, not verified incident locations.

Google's handling is explained in Google's Privacy Policy.

Speed-camera data

Built-in Australian camera locations come from OpenStreetMap and are checked against the phone's location locally. You can also save your own camera spots on the device. Looking up or opening a map for a spot can involve the external services described above.

Camera-location data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence. See OpenStreetMap copyright and licence information and Road Camera's published camera dataset. Viewing these web pages involves the hosting provider. The built-in dataset is not a record of your journeys.

Purchases and local settings

There are no ads and no Useful Llama account or recording-upload server. Google Play handles subscriptions, purchases and payment information. The app queries purchase information and acknowledges purchases to check Unlimited access; it does not receive payment-card details. It keeps local access status and the time of a missing-purchase check, together with recording-use counters, pause timing, report counts, preferences and Terms acceptance. Google holds its purchase records under its own practices.

Storage, retention and deletion

Clips, associated tracks, events, report drafts, photos and PDFs are stored in the app's device storage. Original clips are retained without the free-version overlay; the free plan displays or exports the applicable watermark. Loop recording can replace older unsaved clips. Saved clips are excluded from normal loop replacement. Dismissing an event may return its clips to loop storage.

Use the available delete controls to remove local clips or reports you no longer need. Deleting one record does not guarantee removal of information copied into another report, remembered details, exported files or someone else's copy. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes app-held records and settings. Android's automatic app backup is disabled. Keep needed copies outside the app before removal or reset. The app does not provide cloud recovery of recordings or reports.

Sharing, Gallery and support

When you save to the Gallery, export a file or share through another app, that destination receives the copy you select. Gallery or storage services may sync files to a cloud account under your settings. Recipients may retain or forward copies. Deleting the app's original does not remove those copies.

Send feedback opens your email app with our address and the app name and version in the subject; it does not automatically attach device details, recordings or reports. If no email app is available, it copies our address for you to use.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments through our email provider. Send only what is needed and remove other people's private details where possible. We use correspondence to handle your request and delete it from our mailbox when the issue is resolved; provider retention and backups follow the provider's practices.

Your choices and updates

You can change feature settings and Android permissions. Disabling access can prevent related features from working and does not erase previously saved records or provider-held information. The app is not directed at children. We will update this policy when relevant practices change; the current version and date appear above. Contact useful.llama@gmail.com with privacy questions.
