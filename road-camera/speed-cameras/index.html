Road Camera speed camera locations

Road Camera by Useful Llama includes a list of Australian speed, red-light and average-speed camera locations, so it can note your speed when you pass one.

The list is made from OpenStreetMap data (as of 2026-10-26, 1189 cameras). Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence. This list is available under the same licence.

Download the list (JSON): each entry is latitude, longitude and type (s speed, r red light, a average speed).

Some cameras may be missing or out of date. Volunteers keep OpenStreetMap; you can add or correct cameras there.
