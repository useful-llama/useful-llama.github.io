Fun and Efficient Language — Privacy Policy

Version 1.0. Effective date: 10 November 2026.

Fun and Efficient Language is provided by the independent developer trading as Useful Llama. In this policy, “we” means that developer. Privacy and support contact: useful.llama@gmail.com. The web version is at https://useful-llama.github.io/fun-and-efficient-language/privacy/

What this policy covers

This policy explains the information used by the Android app Fun and Efficient Language. No app account is required. You can study without buying gems or watching ads.

Your learning record

The app saves your settings, lesson and exam results, saved words, your own memory tricks, pinned activities, review schedule, scores, streaks, gem balance, gem jar size and purchase entitlements in the app's private storage on your device. To make sure a gem pack is never credited twice, it also keeps a short code derived from each gem-pack purchase; this code is not the purchase token and cannot be used to identify you or the purchase outside the app. These records make your course, reviews and gems work.

The app does not upload this learning record to a Useful Llama account or server. Android may include it in your Google cloud backup or a device-to-device transfer, depending on your device and backup settings. Those copies are handled by Google under your device settings. This is not an app-managed account or synchronisation service, and a backup restore is not guaranteed.

Speaking and spoken playback

The microphone is used when you start a speaking activity. The app passes recognition to your phone's speech service and uses the recognised words to check an answer or continue a conversation. The app does not itself save an audio recording.

Your speech service may send audio and related information to its provider to recognise your words. Its processing depends on the service and settings on your phone. You can deny or revoke microphone permission in Android settings. Speaking activities offer an alternative to speaking (“Can't talk right now”).

For spoken playback, the app uses an included recording when one is available, otherwise your phone's text-to-speech service, in the accent of the selected Spanish or of the neighbour speaking where your phone offers it. Some voices need an internet connection and may send the text to the voice provider. This can include AI replies you choose to hear. The provider's own privacy policy applies to that processing.

Optional AI conversations

On supported devices, Free talk uses Google's on-device model through ML Kit and Android AICore. The app supplies the lesson context, language preference and recent conversation turns so the model can generate a reply. AI prompts and replies are processed on the device rather than sent to a Useful Llama conversation server. The app does not save Free talk transcripts to its learning record.

On-device AI does not mean all associated services are offline. Speech recognition and playback follow the rules above. Google may download or update models and receive SDK diagnostic and usage information, such as device and app information, identifiers, feature events and performance measurements. See Google's ML Kit terms (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms) and data disclosure (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).

Optional ads

Ads appear only when you choose an ad reward. The app asks for ads rated suitable for teenagers at most. Google AdMob and its applicable advertising providers process information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Google's Mobile Ads SDK documentation describes IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or account identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID, interactions and diagnostic information. The data used depends on your settings and the applicable ad configuration.

Where required, Google's privacy-choice form is shown before ads can load. You can revisit available choices through Ad privacy choices in Settings and use Android's advertising controls. You can keep studying without choosing an ad. See Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure (https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

Purchases

Google Play handles payment for gem packs and gem jars. We do not receive your payment-card details. The app receives the purchase status and product information needed to deliver or restore eligible purchases, and stores the resulting gems and entitlements locally. Google keeps transaction records under its own policies.

If you contact us

If you email support, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to reply, investigate problems and handle purchase or privacy requests. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or sensitive personal information.

We normally delete resolved support correspondence within 12 months of our last substantive reply, unless it is needed for an unresolved dispute or a legal obligation. You can ask for earlier deletion at useful.llama@gmail.com; we will explain anything we must keep. Our email provider may keep copies under its own retention and backup policies.

Your choices and deletion

Your learning record stays in app storage until you remove it. Start the course again (in Settings) resets learning progress, saved words, pins and settings while keeping gems and purchase entitlements; it does not erase everything.

To remove the local app record, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. This also removes locally stored gems. Gem jars bought through Google Play are restored from Play; gems from gem packs already credited are not. Backups may keep or later restore earlier data; manage those in your device or Google account settings. Google Play purchase records and data held by speech, voice, advertising or backup providers are not deleted by the app.

Contact us about access, correction or deletion of information you have sent to Useful Llama. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent or complain to a privacy regulator. We cannot retrieve learning data that exists only on your device, and we will explain when you need to use another provider's controls.

Age

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over, and not for children under 13. Teen users may have additional protections depending on where they live. A parent or guardian can contact us about information a young person has sent to support.

Other services and this website

Google, your speech and voice services, and your backup provider may process information in countries outside your own. Their privacy policies describe their practices and controls.

The policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and request details to provide and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Changes

We will update this policy when the app's practices change and show the new effective date. A new feature that sends lesson or conversation content off the device will be disclosed here before it is introduced.
