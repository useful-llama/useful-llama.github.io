What this policy covers

This policy explains the information used by the Android app Fun and Efficient Language. No app account is required: the optional Google backup is the only sign-in. You can study without it, without buying gems and without watching ads.

Your learning record

The app saves your settings, lesson and exam results, unfinished lessons including your place, queued practice, first-try marks and save time, saved words, your own memory tricks, pinned activities, review schedule, scores, streaks, challenges and trophies, gem balance, gem jar size and purchase entitlements in the app's private storage on your device. It also keeps what you choose to tell the neighbours about yourself in conversations (for example your name, where you are from, your job or studies, what you like, your family and pets), so they can greet you and ask about it later; you can see, edit or forget it on any neighbour's profile page. Tell them only what you are happy to keep in the app. The app keeps a shortened one-way hash of each recently credited gem-pack token to help recognise repeat delivery. It does not keep the original token in the learning record. When Google backup is on, these markers and purchase entitlements are included with your saved record and linked to your app account. These records make your course, reviews and gems work.

Unless you turn on the optional Google backup (below), the app does not upload this learning record to any account or server. Android may include it in your Google cloud backup or a device-to-device transfer, depending on your device and backup settings. Those copies are handled by Google under your device settings. This is not an app-managed account or synchronisation service, and a backup restore is not guaranteed.

Optional Google backup

You can choose “Back up with Google” in Settings, or restore a backup on the first screen. It is never required. When you turn it on, you sign in with your Google account through Android's own sign-in sheet. Google Firebase (a Google service we use for this) then creates a sign-in account for the app, which holds your Google account's email address and a user ID, and stores one copy of your learning record (the information listed above, and the time it was saved) in our Firebase project. Your record can be read and changed by the app only when you are signed in as that user. As the operator of the Firebase project, Useful Llama can technically access the stored records; we do not look at individual backups except to help you when you ask, or where the law requires it. Firebase receives technical information needed to provide the service, such as your IP address. The sign-in tokens are kept in the app's private storage on your device.

While the backup is on, the app saves your record a short time after you study. You can save, restore or stop at any time in Settings. “Stop and delete it all” deletes the cloud copy and the app's Firebase sign-in account. You can also ask us to delete them by emailing useful.llama@gmail.com from, or naming, the Google account you used. See Firebase's privacy information (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

Speaking and spoken playback

Before it uses your phone's speech services, the app asks once (“Use your phone's speech service?”) and names the speech-recognition and text-to-speech apps your phone uses. If you choose “Keep typing”, the app does not use the microphone or your phone's text-to-speech: you answer by typing or with word tiles, and only the app's own recordings play. You can change this at any time in Settings (“Use phone speech”).

The neighbours show your name and the details you told them on screen, but never say them aloud. Replies written by the on-device AI in Charla are shown as text only and are not read aloud.

The microphone is used when you start a speaking activity. The app passes recognition to your phone's speech service and uses the recognised words to check an answer or continue a conversation. The app does not itself save an audio recording.

Your speech service may send audio and related information to its provider to recognise your words. Its processing depends on the service and settings on your phone. You can deny or revoke microphone permission in Android settings. Speaking activities offer an alternative to speaking (“Can't talk right now”).

For spoken playback, the app uses an included recording when one is available, otherwise your phone's text-to-speech service, in the accent of the selected Spanish or of the neighbour speaking where your phone offers it. Some voices need an internet connection and may send the text to the voice provider. This can include replies played automatically, text you tap to hear, remembered personal details and your own chat text. When an online voice is available, the app may select it for playback. With “Play Spanish automatically” off in Settings, conversation and Charla replies are not played by themselves; they play only when you tap them. The provider's own privacy policy applies to that processing.

Optional AI conversations

On supported devices, the app can use Google's on-device model through ML Kit and Android AICore. In guided conversations it can help match an answer to one of the written replies. When generated conversations are enabled, Free talk and Charla can generate replies using the lesson context, language preference, character background, remembered learner details and recent conversation turns. Charla uses AI by default when it is ready and enabled in that build; you can switch AI off in that Charla screen. The model processes these prompts and replies on the device. The app does not retain the whole chat transcript in its learning record, but it can retain facts learned from a conversation and short Charla answer notes. Those saved details are included when Google backup is on.

On-device AI does not mean all associated services are offline. Speech recognition and playback follow the rules above. Google may download or update models and receive SDK diagnostic and usage information, such as device and app information, identifiers, feature events and performance measurements. See Google's ML Kit terms (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms) and data disclosure (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).

Reporting an AI reply

Each AI-written reply in Free talk and Charla has a Report button. If you use it, the app sends the reported reply, the reason you pick, any note you add, and which lesson it was to a private Google Form that only Useful Llama can see (Google Forms stores it for us). No name, email address, account or device identifier is attached to a report, but the reported reply or your note can contain things you told the neighbour, such as your name, so the report may still say something about you. Google receives technical information such as your IP address when the report is sent. We use reports to find and fix problems with AI replies, and delete them once they have been dealt with, normally within 12 months.

Optional ads

Ads are optional rewarded ads: they appear only after you choose an ad reward. While the app has your Unlimited gems entitlement, it offers no ads. For users who choose ads, Google AdMob and its applicable advertising providers process information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. This may include an IP address and approximate location derived from it, device or account identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID, interactions and diagnostics. The data used depends on the applicable settings and ad configuration. The app requests ads with a maximum content rating of Teen.

Where required, Google's privacy-choice form is shown before ads can load. You can revisit available choices through Ad privacy choices in Settings and use Android's advertising controls. You can keep studying without choosing an ad. See Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure (https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

Purchases

Google Play handles payment for gem packs, gem jars and unlimited gems. We do not receive your payment-card details. The app receives the purchase status and product information needed to deliver or restore eligible purchases, and stores the resulting gems and entitlements locally. Google keeps transaction records under its own policies.

If you contact us

If you email support, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to reply, investigate problems and handle purchase or privacy requests. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or sensitive personal information.

We normally delete resolved support correspondence within 12 months of our last substantive reply, unless it is needed for an unresolved dispute or a legal obligation. You can ask for earlier deletion at useful.llama@gmail.com; we will explain anything we must keep. Our email provider may keep copies under its own retention and backup policies.

Your choices and deletion

Your learning record stays in app storage until you remove it. Start the course again (in Settings) resets learning progress, saved words, pins and settings while keeping gems and purchase entitlements; it does not erase everything.

To remove the local app record, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. This also removes locally stored gems, unless you restore them from your Google backup. To delete the Google backup and its sign-in account, use “Stop backing up” → “Stop and delete it all” in Settings, or email us. Gem jars bought through Google Play are restored from Play; gems from gem packs already credited are not. Backups may keep or later restore earlier data; manage those in your device or Google account settings. Google Play purchase records and data held by speech, voice, advertising or backup providers are not deleted by the app.

Contact us about access, correction or deletion of information you have sent to Useful Llama. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent or complain to a privacy regulator. We cannot retrieve learning data that exists only on your device, and we will explain when you need to use another provider's controls.

Age

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over, and not for children under 13. Teen users may have additional protections depending on where they live. A parent or guardian can contact us about information a young person has sent to support.
