What this policy covers

This policy explains the information used by the Android app Funspañol. No app account is required: the optional Google backup is the only sign-in. You can study without it, without buying gems and without watching ads.

Your learning record

The app saves your settings, lesson and exam results, unfinished lessons including your place, queued practice, first-try marks, save time and whether a start gem was paid and can be returned on passing, saved words, your own memory tricks, pinned activities, review schedule, scores, streaks, challenges and trophies, gem balance, gem jar size, purchase entitlements, completed-day unlock progress, extra unlock days earned by watching an ad, the date that unlock-progress ad was last used, which unlock celebrations you have seen and whether an access code was redeemed in the app's private storage on your device. It also saves whether Lola has offered backup, the date and number of reminders, the purchase count at the last reminder and your Don't remind me again choice. It also keeps what you choose to tell the neighbours about yourself in conversations (for example your name, where you are from, your job or studies, what you like, your family and pets), so they can greet you and ask about it later; you can see, edit or forget it on any neighbour's profile page. Tell them only what you are happy to keep in the app. The app keeps a shortened one-way hash of each recently credited gem-pack or Unlock everything bonus purchase token to help recognise repeat delivery. It does not keep the original token in the learning record. When Google backup is on, these markers and purchase entitlements are included with your saved record and linked to your app account. These records make your course, reviews and gems work.

The learning record also stores which neighbours have become friends (friends), a count for each neighbour of your Charla messages that contain a recognised word or phrase from her slang list (slangUses), and which friendship celebrations have already been shown (friendsCelebrated). A qualifying message adds one to her count. The app does not store the message text for this counter; facts and short answer notes remembered through the separate conversation feature are still saved as described above. These records provide friendship progress, open topics and stories, and avoid repeating celebrations. They are included when optional Google backup is on.

The app does not automatically upload this learning record unless you turn on optional Google backup (below). If you choose to share a progress card, its selected details go to the app or recipient you choose, as explained below. Android may include it in your Google cloud backup or a device-to-device transfer, depending on your device and backup settings. Those copies are handled by Google under your device settings. This is not an app-managed account or synchronisation service, and a backup restore is not guaranteed.

Sharing your progress

My stats calculates summaries from the learning record already on your device; it does not add a separate activity-tracking service.

When you choose Share, the app makes a picture card on your phone and opens Android’s share sheet with the card and a message containing the app’s Google Play link. The selected card can include your streak, sellos, open course level, lesson and word-review counts, friendships, trophies or challenges. If you share a certificate, it also includes the name you entered for that certificate.

You choose the destination. The receiving app or service handles the shared content under its own terms and privacy policy. Useful Llama does not automatically receive the card. A temporary copy remains in the app’s cache until replaced by another share or the cache is cleared. Deleting data in this app does not delete copies you have already shared elsewhere.

Optional Google backup

You can choose “Back up with Google” in Settings, from Lola's optional reminder, or restore a backup on the first screen. While backup is off, Lola may offer it after your first completed day or a new purchase. Not now leaves it off; Don't remind me again stops these reminders. The reminder does not itself upload your learning record. Backup is never required. When you turn it on, you sign in with your Google account through Android's own sign-in sheet. Google Firebase (a Google service we use for this) then creates a sign-in account for the app, which holds your Google account's email address and a user ID, and stores one copy of your learning record (the information listed above, and the time it was saved) in our Firebase project. Your record can be read and changed by the app only when you are signed in as that user. As the operator of the Firebase project, Useful Llama can technically access the stored records; we do not look at individual backups except to help you when you ask, or where the law requires it. Firebase receives technical information needed to provide the service, such as your IP address. The sign-in tokens are kept in the app's private storage on your device.

Restoring a Google backup keeps friendships and already-shown friendship celebrations found either in that backup or on this phone. The slang-message counts are taken from the backup rather than added to the phone’s counts; a more recent count may therefore be replaced. This cannot recover a friendship that is absent from both the phone and the saved copy.

While the backup is on, the app attempts to save your record a short time after it changes. Only a successful upload updates the saved copy; connection or service problems can leave it behind your current progress. You can save, restore or stop at any time in Settings. “Stop and delete it all” deletes the cloud copy and the app's Firebase sign-in account. You can also ask us to delete them by emailing useful.llama@gmail.com from, or naming, the Google account you used. See Firebase's privacy information (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

Speaking and spoken playback

Before it uses your phone's speech services, the app asks once (“Use your phone's speech service?”) and names the speech-recognition and text-to-speech apps your phone uses. If you choose “Keep typing”, the app does not use the microphone or your phone's text-to-speech: you answer by typing or with word tiles, and only the app's own recordings play. You can change this at any time in Settings (“Use phone speech”).

In Charla, a neighbour speaks only through an included recording: there is no phone-voice fallback for her lines. Names and remembered details can appear on screen, while any scripted playback uses a generic recorded line without those details. A neighbour-bubble tap and Hear again use the same recorded lines; a line without a matching recording stays text only. AI-written Charla replies are text only. This does not prevent you from tapping your own message to hear it through phone speech, or stop Free talk from reading its AI replies when phone speech is enabled.

The microphone is used when you start a speaking activity. The app passes recognition to your phone's speech service and uses the recognised words to check an answer or continue a conversation. The app does not itself save an audio recording.

Your speech service may send audio and related information to its provider to recognise your words. Its processing depends on the service and settings on your phone. You can deny or revoke microphone permission in Android settings. Speaking activities offer an alternative to speaking (“Can't talk right now”).

For other spoken playback, the app uses an included recording when one is available, otherwise your phone’s text-to-speech service, where phone speech is enabled. The app requests the selected Spanish or character accent where your phone offers it. Some voices need an internet connection and may send the text to the voice provider. This can include replies played automatically, text you tap to hear, remembered personal details and your own chat text. When an online voice is available, the app may select it for playback. With “Play Spanish automatically” off in Settings, spoken conversation and scripted Charla replies do not play by themselves. You can still tap to hear supported lines; a neighbour’s Charla line requires a matching included recording. Charla’s generated AI replies and Ask Lola’s replies remain text only. The provider’s own privacy policy applies to phone-speech processing.

Optional AI conversations

On supported devices, the app can use Google's on-device model through ML Kit and Android AICore. In guided conversations it can help match an answer to one of the written replies. When generated conversations are enabled, Free talk and Charla can generate replies using the lesson context, language preference, character background, remembered learner details and recent conversation turns. Charla uses AI by default when it is ready and enabled in that build; you can switch AI off in that Charla screen. The model processes these prompts and replies on the device. The app does not retain the whole chat transcript in its learning record, but it can retain facts learned from a conversation and short Charla answer notes. Those saved details are included when Google backup is on.

Ask Lola uses your question, the last few messages of that Ask Lola chat, a fixed list of facts about the app and your completed-day count to prepare an answer. When supported on-device AI is ready and generated replies are enabled, that model writes a text answer, which can be general chat or Spanish practice, and may suggest an app screen to open. Otherwise Lola chooses a built-in answer on the device. The app does not send these questions or answers to Useful Llama merely because you use Ask Lola, and it does not save the Ask Lola conversation in the learning record. If you report an answer, the reporting process below applies; an answer can repeat information from your question.

Picture-description practice checks the text you type or the words returned by speech recognition against the lesson's item list. On a supported device, local AI may also compare that text with the item list and produce a short language tip. This feature uses the app's lesson pictures, not photographs taken from your camera or photo library. The app does not send a learner photograph to the AI. Lesson results are saved as described above.

On-device AI does not mean all associated services are offline. Speech recognition and playback follow the rules above. Google may download or update models and receive SDK diagnostic and usage information, such as device and app information, identifiers, feature events and performance measurements. See Google's ML Kit terms (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms) and data disclosure (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).

Reporting an AI reply

AI-written replies in Free talk, Charla and Ask Lola, and AI-written picture-description tips, have a Report button. If you use it, the app sends the reported reply, the reason you pick, any note you add, and the lesson or feature context (such as “Ask Lola”) to a private Google Form operated by Useful Llama. Google Forms stores the submitted report for us. No name, email address, account or device identifier is attached to a report, but the reported reply or your note can contain things you told the neighbour, such as your name, so the report may still say something about you. Google receives technical information such as your IP address when the report is sent. We use reports to find and fix problems with AI replies, and delete them once they have been dealt with, normally within 12 months.

Optional ads

Ads are optional rewarded ads: they appear only after you choose an ad reward. Rewards can be gems or, when offered after your daily goal, an extra day of unlock progress. The app stores the dates and counts needed to apply each offer’s limit in your learning record. While the app has your Unlimited gems entitlement, it offers no ads. For users who choose ads, Google AdMob and its applicable advertising providers process information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. This may include an IP address and approximate location derived from it, device or account identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID, interactions and diagnostics. The data used depends on the applicable settings and ad configuration. The app requests ads with a maximum content rating of Teen.

Where required, Google's privacy-choice form is shown before ads can load. You can revisit available choices through Ad privacy choices in Settings and use Android's advertising controls. You can keep studying without choosing an ad. See Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure (https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

Purchases

Google Play handles payment for gem packs, gem jars, Unlimited gems and Unlock everything. Unlock everything includes a one-time 100-gem bonus; the app saves a recent purchase-credit marker to help avoid crediting the same delivery again. Unlimited gems also opens the neighbours and games. We do not receive your payment-card details. The app receives the purchase status and product information needed to deliver or restore eligible purchases, and stores the resulting gems and entitlements locally. Google keeps transaction records under its own policies.

If you contact us

If you email support, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to reply, investigate problems and handle purchase or privacy requests. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or sensitive personal information.

We normally delete resolved support correspondence within 12 months of our last substantive reply, unless it is needed for an unresolved dispute or a legal obligation. You can ask for earlier deletion at useful.llama@gmail.com; we will explain anything we must keep. Our email provider may keep copies under its own retention and backup policies.

Your choices and deletion

Your learning record stays in app storage until you remove it. Start the course again (in Settings) resets learning progress, saved words, pins and settings while keeping gems and purchase entitlements; it does not erase everything. It keeps friendships, including those obtained with gems, and their celebration history, and clears the slang-message counts. An older backup may later restore information that it still contains.

To remove the local app record, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. This also removes locally stored gems, unless you restore them from your Google backup. To delete the Google backup and its sign-in account, use “Stop backing up” → “Stop and delete it all” in Settings, or email us. The app can restore gem-jar, Unlimited gems and Unlock everything entitlements when Google Play returns valid owned purchases for the same Play account. An already delivered Unlock everything purchase does not grant another 100-gem bonus on ordinary entitlement restore. Play does not restore the remaining balance of already credited gem packs or bonus gems. An available Google backup can restore its saved balance, which replaces the current balance rather than adding to it; more recent gems or progress may be missing. Backups may keep or later restore earlier data; manage those in your device or Google account settings. Google Play purchase records and data held by speech, voice, advertising or backup providers are not deleted by the app.

Contact us about access, correction or deletion of information you have sent to Useful Llama. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent or complain to a privacy regulator. We cannot retrieve learning data that exists only on your device, and we will explain when you need to use another provider's controls.

Age

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over, and not for children under 13. Teen users may have additional protections depending on where they live. A parent or guardian can contact us about information a young person has sent to support.
