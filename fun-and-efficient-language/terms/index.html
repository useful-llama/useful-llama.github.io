Learning, slang and AI

The course is organised into A1–C2 level bands to help you choose practice. Completing a level or skipping an exam is not an accredited qualification or proof of a particular proficiency level.

The neighbours' slang lessons teach informal words used in particular countries. Slang varies between places, ages and situations; use it with friends, not in formal settings.

Speech checks use the words your phone recognised and can misunderstand what you said. They are not a professional assessment of pronunciation. AI conversations, where available, can produce incorrect Spanish, translations, corrections or other inaccurate content. Ask Lola answers questions about the app using built-in facts, with on-device AI where available; her answers and suggested navigation can be wrong. Picture-description practice uses word matching and may use on-device AI to recognise alternative wording and suggest a language tip. These checks are practice aids, not a professional assessment. Use AI for practice and app help, not advice, and do not enter sensitive personal information. Availability depends on your phone, its software and model support.

Gems and retries

Starting a lesson costs 1 gem. If you pass, that start gem is returned, even above your jar size; a same-day retry fee is not refunded. Carrying on a saved lesson costs no additional start gem and keeps any start-gem refund due on passing. A lesson is passed when at least 75% of its checked answers are correct on the first try; each shortcut worksheet row counts as a separate answer. After a failed lesson, a same-day retry costs 2 gems instead of an additional 1-gem start. From the next calendar day according to your device, the usual 1-gem lesson start applies and is returned if you pass. Level exams do not have this lesson-start cost; retrying a failed exam the same day costs 5 gems and is free again the next day. A guided conversation is not passed when fewer than a third of its turns were answered without help; its same-day retry costs 1 gem and is free again the next day. Exams can also be skipped to open the next level without a gem payment or passing reward.

Help costs 1 gem each time: showing the answer or a hint, opening the ideas cards in a conversation, or choosing “Can't talk right now”. When you have no gems, help is free, so you are never stuck.

Fiesta Match boosters have separate gem costs shown before use. Free help at an empty balance does not make a new lesson start, a same-day retry or a booster free. You can wait for an hourly gem refill or use another available gem source. Unlimited gems removes these gem deductions.

Your gems are kept in a gem jar that holds 5 to start. Gems refill at 1 every 60 minutes while the balance is below the jar's size, and refills pause when it is full. Gems you earn by doing well, playing games or watching ads are added only up to the jar's size; earned gems that don't fit are not kept. A bigger gem jar (10 or 20) keeps more earned gems and refills higher.

Gems you buy in a gem pack are always added, even above the jar's size, and are kept until you spend them. Gems have no scheduled expiry in this version, are only for use in this app, and cannot be transferred or exchanged for money.

Optional rewarded ads give the stated reward after the ad provider confirms completion, and may be unavailable. Gem-reward ads are offered only when their gems fit in your jar; the shop reward is 2 gems and games state their own amount. The combined limit for these gem-reward ads is 5 per calendar day. Separately, after meeting today’s stamp goal and while something remains locked, one optional ad per calendar day adds one day of unlock progress. That ad does not award gems and does not depend on jar space. The app offers no ads while you have Unlimited gems.

Unlimited gems is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. While you own it, every neighbour and game is open, lesson starts, retries, help and Fiesta Match boosters do not deduct gems, newly earned gems are not limited by the jar size, and the app offers no ads. The app shows the unlimited symbol instead of a spendable gem count. This purchase is for use in this app and has no cash or transfer value.

Optional Google backup

The Google backup is optional and offered as a convenience. It saves your learning record, including gems, when the app can reach the internet; a backup can be a little behind your latest study. Restoring replaces this phone's learning record and gem balance with the saved copy. It keeps purchase entitlements already on this phone, including Unlock everything and Unlimited gems, the larger jar size and recent purchase-credit markers. Newer progress or gems absent from the backup may be lost; the two gem balances are not added together. Daily-unlock progress and reminder settings come from the saved copy, so newer completed days or preferences may also be replaced. The saved copy also contains ad-earned unlock days, the day that unlock-progress ad was last used, and whether a saved lesson has a refundable start gem. Restoring an older copy can replace newer values. A backup is not a guarantee that all recent data can be recovered. If you delete the backup, it cannot be recovered.

While backup is off, Lola may offer it after your first completed day or a new purchase. You can choose Back up with Google, Not now or Don't remind me again. The reminder itself does not turn backup on. You can still choose backup in Settings later.
