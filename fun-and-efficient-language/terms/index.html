Learning, slang and AI

The course is organised into A1–C2 level bands to help you choose practice. Completing a level or skipping an exam is not an accredited qualification or proof of a particular proficiency level.

The neighbours' slang lessons teach informal words used in particular countries. Slang varies between places, ages and situations; use it with friends, not in formal settings.

Speech checks use the words your phone recognised and can misunderstand what you said. They are not a professional assessment of pronunciation. AI conversations, where available, can produce incorrect Spanish, translations, corrections or other inaccurate content. Use them for practice, not advice, and don't enter sensitive personal information. Availability depends on your phone, its software and model support.

Gems and retries

Lessons have no purchase price. After you fail a lesson, a same-day retry costs 1 gem. After you fail a level exam, a same-day retry costs 3 gems. Guided conversations currently have no retry charge. A retry is free again on the next calendar day according to the device's date. Exams may be skipped to open the next level without a gem payment or passing reward.

Your gems are kept in a gem jar that holds 5 to start. Gems refill at 1 every 30 minutes while the balance is below the jar's size, and refills pause when it is full. Gems you earn by doing well, playing games or watching ads are added only up to the jar's size; earned gems that don't fit are not kept. A bigger gem jar (10 or 20) keeps more earned gems and refills higher.

Gems you buy in a gem pack are always added, even above the jar's size, and are kept until you spend them. Gems have no scheduled expiry in this version, are only for use in this app, and cannot be transferred or exchanged for money.

Optional rewarded ads give the stated reward after the ad provider confirms completion. Ads are not always available, and an ad is offered only when its reward fits in your jar. The shop reward is 2 gems; game offers state their own amount. The combined limit is 5 rewarded ads per calendar day.
