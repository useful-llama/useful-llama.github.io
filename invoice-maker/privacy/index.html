Invoice Maker by Useful Llama — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026 Changes on 12 November 2026: added private feedback, optional thank-yous, Google Play reviews, local checks for other Useful Llama apps, and Android backup and device-transfer details.

Invoice Maker by Useful Llama is provided by the independent developer trading as Useful Llama. In this policy, “we” means that developer. Privacy and support contact: useful.llama@gmail.com.

This policy explains how Invoice Maker handles information. The web version is available at Invoice Maker Privacy Policy.

Information you enter

The app stores information you provide to prepare and manage business documents:

  • Business and client names, addresses, contact details, business identifiers and bank/payment instructions.
  • Invoice types, rates, tax settings, work entries, dates, notes, images, invoices and estimates.
  • Contract wording, party details and signatures you choose to include.
  • Expenses, suppliers, amounts, tax entries and receipt photos.
  • Reports, exported spreadsheets, PDFs, drafts, logos, reusable details and settings.
  • Document allowances, rewarded-reset points, local purchase status and Terms acceptance records.

These records are used to create, display, calculate, organise and export the documents you request. The app does not independently verify the identity of someone whose signature you add. Only enter information or signatures you have authority to use.

Country suggestions, photos and files

The app uses available SIM, network-country and regional settings to suggest country and currency defaults. These suggestions are editable and do not determine your tax status.

When you select a photo, receipt image or file, the app accesses the selected content to attach or import it. App-owned copies can remain with your records after the original is moved or deleted.

Invoice Maker does not offer check-in location recording. Its advertising service can still process IP-based approximate location as described below.

Sharing selected setup details

Share my details creates a file containing the business, client and invoice-type details you select, such as names, addresses, identifiers, email addresses, rates and wording. Bank/payment details require a separate selection and are excluded from Select all. Logos, signatures and device file/folder paths are excluded from this details export.

Importing a selected client or invoice type can update a same-named existing entry. Review the current and shared information before confirming. Replacing your own business details requires a separate choice.

If you put a payment link or QR code on a document, the destination provider processes information when someone opens it. Invoice Maker does not process your clients' card payments.

Optional helpers and reading documents

Smart helpers start switched off and can be changed in Settings. Read receipts uses Google's on-device text recognition when you add an expense photo, suggesting supplier, date, total and tax only for empty fields. Make it professional, available on supported devices and languages, uses Google's on-device AI to rewrite the selected description or email message. Review suggestions before saving or sending; recognition and generated text can be wrong.

Import an invoice or quote and Details from a photo are separate actions that run when you choose a photo, take a photo or select a PDF. Text recognition runs on the phone; PDF reading currently covers only the first three pages. Recognised details can include names, addresses, contact details, business identifiers, bank details, dates and invoice lines. Import creates a draft and may add a client and an imported invoice type. Selected sender details can update your business profile. Details from a photo offers selected values for the profile form, which you review and save. Saved details follow the retention, backup and sharing practices described in this policy. The original document in your chosen storage is not changed.

These features do not send your document content to Useful Llama. Google's text recognition and AI process the supplied content on-device. Model preparation or downloads may require a connection. Google collects SDK usage and diagnostic data, including device and app information, identifiers, performance and feature-event information, under its policies. On-device processing does not mean that no service data leaves the phone. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Turning a helper off prevents its further use through that setting; it does not erase details already saved in records or copies already shared.

Google Play purchases

Google Play handles optional Unlimited yearly subscriptions, one-time purchases and payment information. The app queries purchase and subscription information to check and acknowledge access. It stores a local unlock status and, when needed, the time an ownership check first returned no purchase. It does not retain order IDs or purchase tokens, or receive your payment-card number. Google retains purchase information under its own policies.

Restoring a purchase does not restore your documents. Purchases and backups are separate for Invoice Maker and Time Card.

Optional rewarded ads

You can choose a rewarded ad for three more watermark-free creations when the allowance for that document kind is used. You can continue creating watermarked documents without watching ads. Ads are shown only when you choose the rewarded-ad option. That option is not shown when Unlimited is unlocked.

Google AdMob provides ads. Its Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location, device and account identifiers such as advertising and app-set IDs, interaction information and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This is separate from storing your documents locally. Ad-related approximate location can be inferred from an IP address without device location permission.

Google's User Messaging Platform checks applicable consent requirements and stores privacy choices on the device. For users without Unlimited, a consent-information refresh runs when the app starts after Terms acceptance, and a consent form may appear where required. The ad SDK is initialised and an ad requested when you choose the reward option. Consent checks themselves communicate with Google; not seeing an ad does not mean no information leaves the device.

Where available, Ad privacy choices lets you review or change your choices in How it works or the Unlimited details dialog. Android settings also provide advertising-ID controls. Changing a choice does not automatically delete information already held by Google. See Google's Privacy Policy.

The app stores issued-document counts and rewarded-reset points, which are included in app backups. An ad resets the allowance for the selected document kind only; it does not unlock Unlimited. Separate optional thank-yous are described below.

Private feedback

Private feedback is optional and is separate from a Google Play review. When you press Send, the app sends your rating from 1 to 5, any note you enter (up to 1,000 characters), and the app's name and version to Useful Llama's Google Form over an encrypted connection. The response is stored in Google Forms and its linked response spreadsheet for us to read and use to understand problems and improve the app. Google also records the submission time.

The app does not attach your name, email address, account or device identifier to this request. Your note can identify you if you include personal information, so please leave out names, contact details, passwords and payment information. Google receives connection information when handling the request and processes it under its own privacy policy. This feedback is not a public review and is not published by Useful Llama.

Only Useful Llama's authorised administrators can view the private responses. If monthly reporting is enabled, responses and summary statistics may also be included in a private report emailed to useful.llama@gmail.com. This does not subscribe you to email. Monthly reporting is not required to send feedback.

We retain feedback while using it to investigate issues and plan improvements. There is no automatic deletion schedule for these responses. You can ask us to find, correct or delete a response using the contact details under Support and privacy requests. Our copies in the Form, linked spreadsheet and any retained reports must be handled separately from Google's own service records. Deleting the app or its local data does not delete feedback already sent.

Optional thank-yous and local usage records

The app records when a sharing or private-feedback thank-you was granted, so it can apply its free-document limits. Tell a friend opens Android's sharing options. Android tells the app when you select another app; it does not tell us whether you sent the invitation, who received it or whether anyone installed this app. The selected app's identity is not retained or sent to Useful Llama by this feature. Your chosen sharing app handles the invitation under its own policies.

The app also keeps local counts of days used and attempts to share or email a PDF, the day private feedback was opened, and whether it has requested Google's review card. Opening sharing or email options counts as an attempt even if you later cancel. These records help time optional prompts; they are not sent to Useful Llama as usage analytics.

The app's own structured backup includes document allowances, the sharing-thank-you date, feedback-thank-you date and review-request flag. It does not include the active-day and PDF-share-attempt counters or the feedback-opened day. Android backup can include all of these local allowance and prompt records, as described under Backups and security. A backup you save or share can carry its included records to your selected storage provider or recipient. Clearing local data or uninstalling can remove local records; restoring a backup can restore the records it contains. The app does not use a Useful Llama account to enforce these limits across devices.

Google Play reviews

You can open this app's Google Play page from Rate on Google Play. The app may also ask Google Play to show its own review card after you have used the app for a while. Google decides whether that card appears. Leaving a Play rating or review is optional and never earns a document allowance or other reward.

Google handles ratings and written reviews under its own terms and privacy policy. A review can be public on a publicly released app, while feedback during a closed test is shared privately with the developer. You can manage or delete your Play review through your Google Play or Google Account controls. This app does not receive your selected stars or review text from the review-card flow, or confirmation that you submitted it. It stores only that a review request was made.

Other Useful Llama apps

Where More from Useful Llama is available, the app checks locally whether the listed apps are installed so it can offer Open or a Google Play link. Your phone's language may affect which language-learning app is shown. These checks do not send an installed-app list to Useful Llama. Opening another app or a Play page lets that service handle the visit under its own policies.

Travel received from Road Camera

When you choose to send journeys from Road Camera, Invoice Maker receives journey dates, descriptions containing start and end address summaries and any notes, estimated distance in kilometres, and duration in minutes. This transfer does not include video files or the full GPS route. Invoice Maker shows the travel for review before adding lines to a draft. On confirmation, it stores the accepted travel descriptions and kilometre quantities in the selected invoice type's draft, with the rate you enter. If no suitable manual-line type exists, it creates a Travel type. It also remembers the selected type and rate for later imports. The current distance-based import does not use journey duration to calculate the charge.

Imported lines follow the same storage, backup, export and sharing rules as other invoice information. You can edit or remove them in Invoice Maker. Deleting a source journey in Road Camera does not remove invoice lines or previously exported copies; deleting a line does not remove the source journey. Cancelling the prompt leaves the draft unchanged.

Email, sharing and imported files

When you choose Email, the app passes the attachment, recipient address, subject and proposed message to your chosen email app. You review and send the email there. The email app controls the actual sending account and may process or sync the draft and attachment before you send. This app does not send the email itself or confirm delivery.

CSV and Excel exports contain the selected invoice or time-card records and their included details. You choose whether to save them or open or share them with another service. Exporting a file does not itself connect the app to accounting or payroll software.

Opening, sharing or saving a file gives the receiving app or storage provider access to that copy. A selected folder may belong to a cloud service. Recipients can retain or forward files. Only share information you are authorised to disclose.

Opening a shared details attachment or selecting a file for import lets the app read that file and show its contents for review. A sender name inside a file is not verified identity. No details are added until you confirm the import. Copies held by recipients or external providers follow their own handling and retention practices.

Backups and security

Records are stored in the app's private device storage. No Useful Llama account is required, and the app does not upload your document records to a Useful Llama server for backup. This does not prevent the Google, lookup, feedback, email or other provider processing described in this policy.

Android may back up selected app data through your phone's backup service. On devices using Google's backup service, this is stored in your own Google Account. The app is configured to allow cloud backup only when Android reports support for client-side encryption; with Google's service, the protected app content is encrypted using your device's screen-lock protection. Useful Llama does not receive or hold the keys to that backup content. Availability depends on the device, Android version, backup provider and your settings; a screen lock alone does not mean a backup has completed.

Android backup includes the database and associated journal files: business and client details, addresses and banking or payment information you enter, rates, drafts, issued-document records, expense text and employee or time-card records. It also includes document allowances, local purchase and reviewer-access records, thank-you dates, review-request and prompt-timing records, Terms acceptance and helper preferences. The branding folder, containing the current logo and signature, is included. Purchase ownership remains subject to Google Play checks; restoring app data is separate from restoring a purchase.

Android backup excludes generated PDFs, receipt and attachment photos, saved copies of logos or signatures attached to issued documents, contract-participant signature files, and the check-out reminder and suburb-choice preferences. Location or address text already saved in a business record can still be included with that record. Some PDFs can be recreated from restored records, but this cannot recover missing photos or original signed files. Keep originals and a recent manually saved backup where needed.

Supported Android device-to-device transfers can move the same selected records and files directly to another device. This is separate from cloud backup; support and security depend on the transfer method and device. It is not an ongoing sync service.

To review or turn off cloud backup, search for Backup in your phone's Settings and use your backup provider's controls. Where Google backup is used, its Backup settings show the selected Google Account and backup controls. Menu names vary. Backup timing and storage limits can prevent a recent copy being available. Manage stored backup copies with that provider; deleting local data or uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete those copies.

The app's own Save backup file option creates a ZIP containing structured business records and the included app files currently present, such as PDFs, receipt photos, logos and signatures. It also includes allowances and selected thank-you records, but not every app setting. These ZIP files are not encrypted. Protect your device, store the file somewhere you trust off this phone, and manage any copies you share separately. A manual restore replaces saved records and files and merges allowance and thank-you history; read the confirmation first. A backup cannot recover files already missing when it was made.

Save all PDFs exports available saved PDFs and included receipt images in a ZIP that opens without the app. It is not a backup of editable records. Its READ ME explains omissions and records without a saved PDF. A structured backup requires the matching app to restore.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you remove it with the relevant app controls, replace it during restore, or clear the app's local storage. Uninstalling can remove local data. Keep needed copies first. Android may restore data from an earlier device backup when you reinstall or set up a phone; use the device or backup provider's controls to manage those copies separately.

Deleting a document does not necessarily remove reusable details, settings, allowance counts or Terms acceptance records. It also does not remove previously exported PDFs, backups, email drafts or messages, recipient copies or records held by external services. Manage those copies separately with their providers.

If you decline changed Terms, you can still view and share saved PDFs, export them and save a structured backup without accepting the new Terms or buying an upgrade. Drafts and records without saved PDFs are not displayed in that read-only viewer.

Support and privacy requests

Contact useful.llama@gmail.com with support or privacy questions. If you email us, we and our email provider receive your email address, message and attachments to handle the request. Include only information needed to resolve it. We keep support emails until the issue is resolved, then delete them from our mailbox. Our email provider may retain copies under its own retention and backup policies.

You can ask about access, correction or deletion of information we hold by emailing us. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent or complain to a privacy regulator. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase records held only on your device, or withdraw copies held by recipients.

For private feedback, email useful.llama@gmail.com with the app name, approximate submission date and time, and a short exact excerpt from your note, if you supplied one. This helps us locate the response without a device identifier. We may need more details to distinguish it from similar responses. Do not send passwords or payment details. We can handle copies we control in the feedback Form, linked spreadsheet and retained reports; Google controls its own service records. Deleting a response does not recall copies already received by others. Requests about a Google Play review should use Google's review controls.
