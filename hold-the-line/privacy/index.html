Crimson Defiance: Hold the Line by Useful Llama — Privacy Policy

Changes in 1.2: the optional cloud save and achievements through Google Play Games. Changes in 1.1: optional private feedback through Google Forms, and the one-time gems thank-you for it. Crimson Defiance: Hold the Line ("the game") is a tower defence game for Android provided by the independent developer trading as Useful Llama. In this policy, “we” means that developer. Privacy and support contact: useful.llamita@gmail.com.

This policy explains what the game does with information. In short: there is no Useful Llama account and no Useful Llama server that stores your game; your game stays on your phone unless you sign in to Google Play Games, which then keeps a copy with your Google account; Google handles the optional videos and the optional purchases; and an optional gift code is checked by Useful Llama's code service.

Your game on your phone

The game saves your progress in its own private storage on your phone: your gems, the maps you have opened and your best wave and stars on each, your Armoury levels, awards, daily gift days, how many videos you have watched today, the side and map you last chose, your settings, and which Google Play products you own. To recognise a purchase it has already given you, it keeps a short one-way code made from Google Play's purchase receipt, never the receipt itself. If you redeem a gift code, the game also keeps the gift's grant reference, receipt and redemption time, a randomly generated installation identifier and a note that the gift's gems were given, apart from your purchases; it never keeps the code itself. A run in progress is not saved.

So that a loading problem can be explained if you ask us for help, the game also keeps a short technical record of its own loadings on the phone (for example how far a loading got and how much memory the phone had). That record stays on the phone; the game never sends it anywhere.

No Useful Llama account is needed and we do not run a server that stores your game. The game does not ask for your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or location.

Dogecoin and gems

Dogecoin in this game is only the in-game coin you build with during a run. It is not cryptocurrency, it is never sold, and it never leaves the game. Gems are the game's premium currency: they are earned in play or bought through Google Play, are for this game only, and have no cash value.

Optional videos (rewarded ads)

The game never shows an ad you did not choose: there are no banners and no ads between screens, no ads during play, and no video is offered before your first game. You may choose to watch a video for a few gems in the shop, or to continue a lost run once. Google AdMob provides those videos. When you choose one, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information such as your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID, ad interactions and diagnostic information, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Ads are limited to those rated suitable for teenagers.

Google's User Messaging Platform checks whether your consent is needed where you live and, if so, shows Google's consent form first and stores your choices on the phone. Where Google asks for it, the shop has AD PRIVACY CHOICES to review them. Android's settings let you reset or delete your advertising ID. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Google Play purchases

Gem packs, the Starter Pack and Unlock Everything can be bought through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment and your payment details; the game never receives your card number or payment credentials. The game asks Google Play for the prices and for the purchases on your Google account, so it can give you what you bought, finish the purchase with Google Play, and restore once-only purchases (RESTORE PURCHASES). Google keeps purchase records under its own policies. Gems already given are not given again by a restore.

Optional gift codes

If you choose to redeem a gift code, the app sends the code, the app identifier and a randomly generated installation identifier to Useful Llama's code service on Google Apps Script. No Useful Llama account is required. The installation identifier lets the service recognise a repeated request and manage the redemption limit; it is not your name, but it can link requests from this installation. Checking an existing gift sends its grant reference and the same installation identifier instead of the code.

Useful Llama keeps redemption records in a private Google spreadsheet, including the app, installation identifier, grant reference, receipt, redemption time, code version, allocated place, grant status and notification status. The service's code list stores salted hashes rather than readable codes. Redemption notifications sent to the Useful Llama owner include the app, reward, receipt, time and redemption counts. They do not include the code or installation identifier. Google processes service and connection information under its own policies.

These records provide and check the gift, prevent repeated requests from consuming another place, enforce the redemption limit and manage owner notifications. Uninstalling the app does not delete the service's redemption records or emails. To ask for access to your redemption record or for its removal, write to useful.llamita@gmail.com with the app name and the receipt or approximate redemption time. Do not send the gift code, private recovery reference or passwords. When a record is removed, its installation identifier is deleted and the redemption still counts towards the code's limit. The gift can no longer be checked or restored from the service: a later check on the phone turns off the maps and Armoury levels it opened, but gems already given stay in the game, the same as gems earned in play. We may need further information to locate the record. Google controls its own service records.

Optional feedback

If you choose Send feedback, the game sends your 1–5 star answer, your optional note and the game's name and version to a private Google Form operated by Useful Llama, which emails each answer to Useful Llama's inbox. This is feedback to the developer, not a Google Play rating or review, and you can play without ever sending it. The game does not attach your name, email address, account or any device identifier. Your note could still contain personal information, so please leave out passwords, payment details and other sensitive information.

Google processes the submission, and technical connection information such as your IP address, under its own policies. We use feedback to find problems and improve our apps. Answers are kept in Google Forms, a linked Google Sheet and a monthly internal report emailed to Useful Llama; they are not published. We keep them while we need them to act on them, normally no longer than twelve months. To have feedback found, corrected or deleted, email us with the game's name, the approximate date and enough of your note to find it. Uninstalling the game does not delete feedback already sent.

The first feedback that reaches the form adds 100 gems once, whatever stars you choose. The save on your phone records that this thank-you was given, so it is not given twice; it is never tied to a Google Play rating.

Other Useful Llama apps

Settings can show a list of other Useful Llama apps with a link to each one's Google Play page. The game does not check which apps you have installed. Opening a link lets Google Play handle the visit under its own policies. Nothing is given for installing another app.

Cloud save and achievements (Google Play Games)

Cloud save. If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game keeps a copy of your progress (gems, upgrades, map records, awards and settings) in your Google Play Games saved games, so it can be brought back on another phone. Google stores this copy with your Google account; Useful Llama does not receive it. You can delete it in Google Play Games' settings, and the game works without signing in.

Achievements. When you are signed in, the awards you earn in the game (for example First Blood or a map's Veteran award, and the stars) are also unlocked as Google Play Games achievements on your Play Games profile, where Google shows them under its own settings. Awards earned before you signed in are unlocked when you sign in.

Signing in is optional; the game offers it and remembers nothing about your account beyond the Play Games name it shows in Settings. Google Play Games processes your sign-in, saved games and achievements under the Google Privacy Policy.

Backups

Android may include the game's save in your phone's own backup or a transfer to a new phone (for example to your Google account), depending on your phone and settings. That backup also holds a gift code's information (the installation identifier, grant reference and receipt), so a phone restored from it keeps the gift without using the code again. That backup is handled by your phone's backup provider; we do not receive it.

Children

Crimson Defiance: Hold the Line is a war game meant for players aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used a feature that sent information, contact us and we will help.

Keeping and deleting information

Information on your phone stays until you delete it: uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, removes your save and its technical record. Copies in your phone's backup are managed in that service, and the cloud save and achievements in Google Play Games' settings. Purchases stay on your Google account and can be restored after reinstalling. Gift-code redemption records are kept and removed as described under Optional gift codes. If you email us, we keep the email until your question is resolved, normally deleting it within twelve months.
